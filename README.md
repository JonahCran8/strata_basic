# Strata Basic

The layered world of Strata, and nothing else (NeoForge 1.21.1, may bring to other versions if I have time)

- **Layers** (top to bottom): stone (above Y 0), deepslate (0 to -100), lithoslate (-100 to -200), mantleslate (-200 to -300), ferrite
  (-300 to -400), and the Nether roof starts below -400, and is about 15-20 blocks thick, under which the Nether can be found.
  The Nether takes up the bottom 128 blocks of the world (Y -528 to -400), and the total world size is Y -528 to 256.
  The deeper world costs a little FPS, which is mainly noticeable if you fly in spectator mode with a high render distance.
- **Stones:** lithoslate, mantleslate and ferrite are deepslate with changed colour palette, come with a variant of every ore and can be crafted into everything deepslate can be
  crafted into (cobbled, polished, bricks, cracked bricks, tiles, cracked tiles, chiseled, and their slabs, stairs and walls). Mining them
  drops its cobbled block.
- **Ores:** coal, iron, copper, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond and emerald generate in every layer, in the layer's own stone.
- **Creepers:** Deeper Creeper(deepslate), Lithoslate Creeper, Mantleslate Creeper and Void Creeper(ferrite), each has a bigger blast and spawns naturally in its own
  layer (regular creepers spawn in the stone layer).
- **The Nether:** Nether fortresses and bastions generate, as well as gold, quartz, and debris.
  
Everything else is vanilla. The mod replaces the overworld's height, noise settings and biome source, so it conflicts with other mods that overhaul world generation. Don't use it together with full Strata. World creation skips Minecraft's "experimental settings" warning
